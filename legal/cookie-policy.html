What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Under Dutch law (Telecommunicatiewet, Art. 11.7a) and the EU ePrivacy Directive, we are required to inform you about the cookies we use and to obtain your prior consent before placing any non-essential cookies on your device.

Cookies We Use

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function correctly. They do not store personally identifiable information and do not require your consent under Art. 11.7a Telecommunicatiewet.

We do not currently embed any third-party content on this website. If we introduce embeds in the future (such as Instagram feeds or WhatNot widgets), those platforms may set cookies on our domain, which we will disclose and seek consent for. We will update this policy and our cookie banner accordingly before introducing any such embeds.

Managing and Withdrawing Consent

You may update your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings icon at the bottom left of this website. Withdrawing consent is a single-step action and takes immediate effect. Withdrawing consent does not affect any processing that took place while consent was in force.

You may also manage or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functioning of this website. For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your cookie consent correctly, you may lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl, +31 (0)70 888 8500.
