What Data We Collect, Why, and on What Legal Basis

This website is a collection showcase with no registration, checkout, or contact forms. We collect a minimal amount of data when you visit:

  • Server logs — your IP address, browser type, pages visited, referrer URL, and timestamp. These are retained for up to 30 days and then automatically deleted. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our legitimate interest is to maintain the security, performance, and technical integrity of our website and to diagnose faults. We have assessed that this interest does not override your rights and freedoms, given the limited nature of the data collected, the short retention period, and the absence of any profiling or automated decision-making.
  • Cookie consent record — when you interact with our cookie banner, your consent preferences are stored locally in your browser via a Cookiebot cookie. This is a strictly necessary function and does not require separate consent. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or any other third-party tracking tools on this website. We do not collect your name, email address, or payment information through this website. If you purchase via WhatNot, your data is processed by WhatNot under their own privacy policy.

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — to receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — to have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — to request deletion of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — to restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — where we process your data on the basis of legitimate interest, you have the right to object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@epic-decks.com. We will respond within one calendar month of receipt, in accordance with Art. 12 GDPR. For complex or multiple requests this period may be extended by a further two months, in which case we will notify you within the first month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl, +31 (0)70 888 8500.
